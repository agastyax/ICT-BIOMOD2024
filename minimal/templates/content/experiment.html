Synthesis of Magnetite Nanoparticles
Synthesis of Magnetite Nanoparticles Magnetite nanoparticles (MNPs)were synthesized using the co-precipitation method. FeCl2 and FeCl3 were taken in a ratio of 1:2 in a tri-necked flask and dissolved in a sufficient amount of water, it was then magnetically stirred under inert conditions to prevent oxidation of Fe2+ into Fe3+ and therefore changing the ratio. NaOH was added dropwise to the Iron solution till a pH of 10 to obtain a black colloidal suspension of magnetite nanoparticles. The suspension was heated at 65C for 10 mins.
